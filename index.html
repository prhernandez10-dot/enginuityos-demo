Operating platform for the aviation aftermarket

ERP manages transactions. EnginuityOS governs the decisions.

Buying, repairing and selling aircraft parts and engines is one loop. EnginuityOS records every decision in it, makes sure the right person makes it, watches the ERP without touching it, and keeps a record nobody can quietly change. AI advises; people decide.

Repair can come before stock or after a sale, a teardown turns one asset into many lots, and review feeds the next demand: the stream is a loop.

The six rules the platform never breaks

  • G1Nothing is overwritten. Every fact is saved as a new record in a chain that shows any tampering.
  • G2When in doubt, refuse. A missing permission or setting stops the action instead of guessing.
  • G3One writer per record. Two people can't silently overwrite each other.
  • G4Replayable. The full state can be rebuilt from the saved records and comes out identical.
  • G5Companies are sealed off. One customer can never see or touch another's data.
  • G6AI never decides. AI can recommend; only an accountable person can approve.

How this demo works

The parts and Data Center walkthroughs run the platform's own code in your browser. The engine walkthrough replays a recorded run. What differs from a server is listed here.

What runs

  • The material ledger: packages, serial-numbered units, purchase orders with approval limits, receipts and holds, repair orders, alert tasks, quality acceptance, reservations, sale, exposure and the recovery review.
  • The lot ledger: lots of consumables received against a purchase order, permitted uses, shelf life, allocation, issue, consumption and returns, with a conservation check.
  • The Daily ERP Data Center: sources, approved mappings, content-addressed intake, validation, reconciliation, atomic publication, history and freshness, fed by the platform's synthetic ERP generator.
  • The governed deal workflow (recorded): deals, verified evidence, compliance, approval gates, award, custody, settlement, title, the executed outcome with per-asset amounts, record lock and replay. The stored records' hash chain is verified live.
  • The kernel and journal: the append-only event record, SHA-256 hash chain and AES-256-GCM encryption, the same code a server uses.
  • Identity rules: every action is checked against role, company, and whether the actor is a human, a service or an AI.

What differs from a server

  • Records live in memory in your browser, in the platform's own in-memory SQL client, instead of PostgreSQL. Reloading clears them.
  • Hashing and encryption use audited open-source JavaScript libraries and a fixed demonstration key.
  • Everyone is a role, not a real sign-in. A real deployment uses the company's Microsoft accounts.
  • Rules and limits come from the platform's synthetic test policy plus demo purchase limits. A real customer sets its own.
  • Dates in the walkthroughs are fixed so every run gives the same result.

How it fits an existing ERP

  • The ERP stays the system of record. EnginuityOS reads its daily exports and never writes to it. Nothing is replaced or re-entered.
  • What varies by company is configuration, not code. Roles, approval limits, alert rules, required documents and column mappings are per-customer settings, versioned and approved by a second person.
  • Less administration, not more. Alerts are raised by the daily check, tasks close when their condition is met, and refused attempts log themselves.

What it does not show

The operator console, engine valuation, the fleet data service and live ERP connections run on the server and are not in this page.
